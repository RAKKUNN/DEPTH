FEATURES
⛏

REAL-TIME DRILLING

Tap to drill through Earth's geological layers — from the thin crust all the way through the mantle, outer core, and inner core. Manage drill temperature and pressure as you descend 6,371 km.

💎

36 MINERALS

Discover and collect 36 scientifically accurate minerals found at each layer of Earth. From common quartz to ultra-rare perovskite and iron-nickel alloys deep in the core.

🔧

DRILL UPGRADES

Upgrade your drill's motor power, cooling system, drill bit material, and rotation speed. Each upgrade opens new depths and increases mining efficiency.

🔄

MINERAL EXCHANGE

Synthesize lower-tier minerals into higher-tier ones. Trade 5 common minerals to obtain 1 rare mineral from the next tier through the exchange system.

🌍

ANTIPODE DISCOVERY

Upon completion, the app uses your real GPS coordinates to calculate and visualize the exact antipodal point — the location on Earth directly opposite to you.

📜

COMPLETION CERTIFICATE

Receive a personalized certificate showing your departure point, arrival coordinates, completion time, and total taps. Save and share your achievement.

PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: May 2026

1. DATA COLLECTION

DEPTH. ("the App") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All game progress is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage.

2. LOCATION DATA

The App requests access to your device's GPS coordinates solely to calculate the antipodal point upon game completion. This data is:

  • Processed entirely on-device
  • Never transmitted to any external server
  • Never stored beyond the current session
  • Used only for the in-game antipode visualization feature

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App may use OpenStreetMap's Nominatim API for reverse geocoding (converting coordinates to a readable place name). This request contains only latitude and longitude — no personal identifiers are sent.

4. ANALYTICS & TRACKING

The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks. No cookies are used. No user profiles are created.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The App contains no advertisements, in-app purchases, or social features.

6. CONTACT

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@earthcore.app.
